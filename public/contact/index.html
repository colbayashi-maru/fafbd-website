What this page does not say

Investing involves risk, including the possible loss of the amount you invest. No investment strategy assures a profit or protects against loss in a declining market, and nothing on this site is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Securities products are not FDIC insured, carry no bank guarantee and may lose value. SIPC protects the custody of securities and cash if a brokerage fails; it does not protect against market loss.

We are paid by commission when you buy or sell. We make more when there are more transactions, which is a conflict of interest between us and you. We do not monitor accounts on an ongoing basis unless we have separately agreed in writing to do so. Our Form CRS sets out our services, our costs, our conflicts and our disciplinary history, and we encourage you to read it before you open an account.

Annuities and variable life policies are long-term contracts. They carry surrender charges for early withdrawal, ongoing contract and rider fees, and tax consequences on early distribution — and the guarantees in them depend on the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company, not on First Asset Financial and not on SIPC. All are sold by prospectus; read it, including the charges and expenses, before you invest. First Asset Financial does not provide tax or legal advice.

Securities products are not FDIC insured, carry no bank guarantee, and may lose value, including loss of the amount invested. SIPC protects the custody of securities and cash at a failed brokerage; it does not protect against a decline in the market value of an investment.

First Asset Financial is not a registered investment adviser and does not provide investment advisory, tax or legal advice. Some of our representatives are separately licensed as investment adviser representatives of an unaffiliated or affiliated advisory firm; that is a different relationship, with different costs and different obligations. Our Form CRS describes both, along with the conflicts of interest that apply to us.

First Asset Financial is a limited-scope broker-dealer. Our securities business is variable annuities, registered index-linked annuities and variable universal life. We do not offer mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, stocks, bonds, municipal securities, options or any other individual security. The scope of our registration is on the public record at FINRA BrokerCheck.
